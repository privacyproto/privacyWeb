EyeState Privacy Policy

App Name: EyeState
Effective Date: August 17, 2026

EyeState is a personal utility that helps you manage screen time and build healthier device-use habits. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information related to your use.

1. App Features

EyeState provides the following features:

  • Screen time statistics, history, and trend analysis
  • App restrictions based on daily limits, schedules, locations, and other conditions
  • Quick limits, Child Mode, and related reminders
  • Vision and eye-health support tools
  • Device restrictions for app installation, removal, and in-app purchases
  • Flip or shake shortcuts for returning to the Home Screen
  • App Lock using Face ID, Touch ID, or a gesture
  • Premium subscriptions purchased through the App Store

EyeState is intended for wellness and habit-management support only. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

2. How Information Is Handled

The following information is stored only on your device or in the app's local App Group storage and is not uploaded to the developer's servers:

  • Onboarding questionnaire answers and preferences
  • Screen time statistics, wellness scores, and related records
  • Usage rules and your selected apps, categories, and websites
  • Locations saved for location-based rules
  • Tool test results and history
  • App settings such as language, appearance, and notification preferences
  • Subscription status verified by the App Store and cached locally

EyeState does not require account registration, does not send analytics or tracking data to developer servers, and does not actively collect your legal name, government ID, or similar identity information.

3. Permissions

With your consent, EyeState may request the following permissions to provide its features:

  • Screen Time: access system-provided usage statistics and enforce app restrictions you configure
  • Location: trigger location-based rules when you arrive at or leave a saved place
  • Face ID / Touch ID: authenticate App Lock; biometric data is handled by the system and is not accessible to the app
  • Notifications: deliver local break and rule reminders
  • Motion sensors: detect flip or shake shortcuts

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Apple System Services

  • Subscription products, payments, and purchase restoration are processed by Apple StoreKit and governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • When you use place search or address resolution, your query and location may be processed by Apple Maps and system location services.
  • System reviews, notifications, Screen Time, and biometric authentication are provided by iOS.

The developer does not receive your payment details, biometric data, or complete Screen Time activity through these services.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The app does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

6. Data Management

You can use the app's available controls to export or delete local data. You can also remove data in the app container by uninstalling the app. System permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Child Mode and screen restrictions are configured locally by the device user or a guardian. EyeState does not require children to create accounts and does not upload children's personal information to developer servers.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when app features or legal requirements change. An updated version will be provided with an app update and will identify its new effective date.
