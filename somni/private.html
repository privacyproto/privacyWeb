1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Somni (眠迹, referred to as the “App”) handles information when you use its overnight sound monitoring, sleep reporting, menstrual cycle recording, and cycle trend estimation features.

The App is provided by Somni, referred to in this policy as “we,” “us,” or “our.”

Somni is designed primarily as an on-device application. Information handled locally by the App is not transmitted to or collected by us unless this policy expressly states otherwise.

2. Information handled by the App

2.1 Overnight audio recordings

When you start an overnight recording session and grant microphone permission, Somni may record audio through your device's microphone. Recording may continue while the screen is locked so that the App can monitor the actual duration of your sleep session.

The App analyzes audio to identify possible sound events, including:

  • Snoring
  • Sleep talking
  • Teeth grinding
  • Coughing
  • Other environmental or unclassified sounds

Audio recordings and detected sound events are stored in the App's local storage on your device. Sound identification is automated and may not always be accurate.

2.2 Sleep session information

The App may locally store information such as:

  • Recording start and end times
  • Actual overnight recording duration
  • Sound-event timestamps and durations
  • Playback position and recording recovery checkpoints
  • Bedtime factors selected by you
  • Morning energy, mood, symptoms, and notes

If the device loses power or the App is interrupted during a recording, Somni may use the last locally recorded checkpoint to estimate the end of the session and preserve the available recording.

2.3 Information you enter

You may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Body weight
  • Menstrual period start and end dates
  • Daily factors that may be related to snoring
  • Morning reflections, symptoms, or notes

This information is stored locally and is used to provide reports, comparisons, and personalized trends inside the App.

2.4 Apple Health information

If you explicitly grant permission, Somni may read the following categories of information from Apple Health:

  • Sleep analysis
  • Heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (SDNN)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Sleeping wrist temperature
  • Menstrual flow records

Somni requests only the Health permissions required for the features you choose to use. The App does not write menstrual, sleep, or other health records back to Apple Health.

2.5 App settings and technical information

The App may store preferences and operational information locally, such as selected settings, permission status, onboarding status, display preferences, and the last known state of an active recording.

Somni does not use third-party advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or behavioral analytics SDKs.

2.6 Face ID and device authentication

If you enable Face ID protection, authentication is performed by iOS. Somni receives only the result of the authentication attempt. The App does not receive, access, or store your facial image or biometric template.

2.7 Information provided when contacting support

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, message, attachments, and any information you voluntarily provide. We use this information only to respond to your request, provide support, investigate problems, or comply with legal obligations.

3. How information is used

Information handled by Somni may be used to:

  • Record and play back overnight audio
  • Display a timeline of sound activity during the night
  • Identify possible snoring, sleep talking, grinding, and coughing
  • Create local sleep and sound summaries
  • Compare snoring patterns with factors such as body weight
  • Display sleep and physiological trends from Apple Health
  • Record menstrual period dates entered by you or read with permission
  • Estimate a possible future menstrual period window
  • Restore an interrupted recording from its last available checkpoint
  • Protect local records using device authentication
  • Maintain App preferences and functionality

We do not use your health information, audio recordings, or menstrual cycle information for advertising, marketing, data brokerage, or profiling by third parties.

4. Apple Health and HealthKit

Access to Apple Health information is optional and requires your explicit authorization through Apple's permission interface.

HealthKit information is used only to provide health-related App features, including:

  • Displaying sleep and overnight physiological trends
  • Showing wrist temperature trends when available
  • Supplementing menstrual cycle records
  • Estimating a possible next menstrual period window

Somni does not sell HealthKit information and does not use it for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, insurance decisions, or other unrelated purposes.

The App does not share HealthKit information with advertising platforms, data brokers, or analytics providers.

You can review or revoke Somni's Health permissions at any time in the Apple Health app or in iOS Settings. Revoking access prevents the App from reading new Health information but does not automatically delete information already stored locally by the App.

5. Local storage and security

Somni stores App records, audio recordings, sound-event results, and imported Health summaries locally inside the App's container on your device.

Somni does not operate a cloud account or backend service for uploading these records. We therefore cannot remotely access your locally stored audio or health records.

The App uses security protections provided by iOS and may allow you to protect access with Face ID or device authentication. However, no storage or security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Depending on your device and Apple backup settings, locally stored App data may be included in system-managed device backups. Such backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Somni.

This privacy policy webpage itself does not use cookies, analytics, advertising technology, or external scripts.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, health information, menstrual cycle information, or audio recordings.

Because the App's primary data processing takes place locally, we do not normally receive or possess your App records and cannot disclose data that has not been transmitted to us.

Information may be disclosed only in limited circumstances:

  • When you intentionally export or share information using system sharing features
  • When necessary to respond to a support request containing information you voluntarily provided
  • When required to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, but only for information actually held by us
  • When necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or the App

Apple may separately process information through iOS, HealthKit, device backups, App Store services, and other Apple services under Apple's own privacy policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete the relevant record, use the App's delete-all-data option, or uninstall the App.

You may delete information by:

  • Deleting individual recordings or records where the App provides that option
  • Using the delete-all-data function in the App's settings
  • Uninstalling the App from your device

Deleting Somni does not delete the original information stored in Apple Health because Somni reads Health information but does not control the Apple Health database. You can manage Apple Health records directly in the Health app.

Copies contained in system-managed device backups may remain until those backups expire or are deleted according to your Apple backup settings.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

8. Your permissions and choices

You remain in control of the permissions granted to Somni.

Microphone

You can revoke microphone access in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Microphone. Overnight recording will not work without microphone permission.

Apple Health

You can review or revoke Health access in the Apple Health app or in iOS Settings. Menu names may vary depending on your iOS version.

Face ID

You can disable Face ID protection within the App, where available, or manage the App's Face ID permission in iOS Settings.

Local records

You can review and delete supported records within the App. Because records are stored locally, we generally cannot access, recover, export, or delete them remotely on your behalf.

9. Health and medical disclaimer

Somni is intended for personal awareness and general wellness purposes. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency monitoring.

Sound classifications are automated estimates. A detected sound may be incorrectly classified, and actual snoring, sleep talking, teeth grinding, coughing, or other conditions may not be detected.

Menstrual period predictions are approximate estimates based on the information available to the App, including recorded cycle dates and, when authorized and available, wrist temperature trends. Predictions may be inaccurate due to incomplete data, irregular cycles, illness, stress, medication, pregnancy, hormonal changes, device usage, or other factors.

Do not use Somni's cycle estimates as contraception, fertility treatment guidance, or confirmation of pregnancy. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have health concerns or need medical guidance.

10. Children's privacy

Somni is not directed to children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by applicable local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online service.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.
